❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is PCOS and how common is it? +

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder affecting 1 in 10 women of reproductive age. It's characterized by irregular periods, excess androgen levels, and polycystic ovaries. Early diagnosis and management can help control symptoms and reduce long-term health risks.

What are the main symptoms of PCOS? +

Common symptoms include irregular or missed periods, excess hair growth (hirsutism), acne, weight gain, thinning hair on the scalp, darkening of skin, and difficulty getting pregnant. Not all women experience all symptoms.

Can diet really help manage PCOS? +

Yes! A balanced diet can significantly improve PCOS symptoms. Focus on low-GI foods, lean proteins, healthy fats, and plenty of vegetables. Limiting processed foods and sugar helps manage insulin resistance, a key factor in PCOS.

What type of exercise is best for PCOS? +

A combination of cardio and strength training works best. Aim for 150 minutes of moderate exercise weekly. Activities like brisk walking, swimming, cycling, and resistance training help improve insulin sensitivity and hormone balance.

Can PCOS be cured? +

PCOS cannot be completely cured, but symptoms can be effectively managed through lifestyle changes, medications, and regular monitoring. Many women successfully control their symptoms and lead healthy lives with proper management.

How does PCOS affect fertility? +

PCOS is one of the leading causes of infertility due to irregular ovulation. However, many women with PCOS can conceive with proper treatment, lifestyle modifications, or fertility treatments. Consult a specialist for personalized guidance.
